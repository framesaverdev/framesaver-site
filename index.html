FrameSaver

What FrameSaver does

FrameSaver picks the sharpest, best-lit frames from your videos and saves them as full-resolution photos, right on your phone.

Privacy policy

FrameSaver does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything happens on your device:

  • Videos you choose are processed locally — nothing is uploaded anywhere
  • Face detection (used to help pick good shots) runs entirely on-device via Google's ML Kit, which does not send images off the phone
  • No account, sign-in, or personal information is required to use the app
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind

Saved photos go to your device's own Pictures/FrameSaver folder, just like any photo you'd take with the camera.
